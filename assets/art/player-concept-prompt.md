## 正向提示词

年轻男性和风剑士角色设定，全身像，冷静沉着的水系月刃武士，深海蓝、黑色、银白和暗金为主色调。角色身形修长敏捷，肤色白皙，深蓝近黑的蓬松长发扎成高马尾，额前有凌乱刘海，眼神锐利安静，表情克制。穿黑色内衬和宽松袴裤，外披深海蓝短袖羽织，羽织有高立领、白色滚边、暗金扣饰，衣摆和袖口绘有银白与浅蓝色浪纹图案。腰间有黑金腰带、半月形金属装饰、红色结绳和流苏挂饰。手腕、护臂、小腿和靴面带有黑色皮革护具、暗金铆钉和月牙纹饰。脚穿黑色忍者风绑带靴。

武器是一把细长武士刀，刀身发出冰蓝色光芒，挥砍时形成弯月形水流剑气，周围有水花碎片、蓝白飞溅粒子和冷光轨迹。整体气质是夜色竹林中的清冷剑客，主题关键词为水、月、浪、速度、精准斩击。角色适合横版动作像素游戏主角，设计需要兼顾站立待机、冲刺斩、跳跃斩和大范围水浪斩击动作。

画面风格为高精度 2D 像素艺术角色概念图，清晰像素边缘，精细布料层次，金属高光明确，暗色背景，低饱和夜色氛围，蓝白水光作为主要视觉焦点，角色轮廓干净，可读性强，适合拆分为游戏角色立绘、动作帧和技能特效素材。

## 英文提示词

Young male Japanese-inspired swordsman character, full body, calm water-and-moon themed warrior, deep navy blue, black, silver white, and muted gold color palette. Slim agile build, pale skin, dark blue almost black long tousled hair tied in a high ponytail, sharp quiet eyes, restrained serious expression. Black inner robe and loose hakama pants, deep navy short-sleeved haori with a high collar, white trim, muted gold fasteners, silver and pale blue wave patterns on the sleeves and lower hem. Black and gold belt with a crescent moon ornament, red cord knot and tassel accessories. Black leather wrist guards, bracers, shin guards, strapped boots, muted gold rivets and crescent details.

He wields a slender katana with an icy blue glowing blade. Each slash creates crescent-shaped water sword trails, splashing blue-white droplets, cold light particles, and flowing wave arcs. The character feels like a silent night swordsman in a bamboo grove, built around water, moonlight, waves, speed, and precise cutting attacks. Suitable as the protagonist of a side-scrolling action pixel game, with readable idle stance, dash slash, jumping slash, and large water-wave attack poses.

High-detail 2D pixel art character concept sheet, crisp pixel edges, detailed layered fabric, clear metallic highlights, dark background, low-saturation night atmosphere, blue-white water glow as the main focal effect, clean silhouette, strong readability, suitable for character portrait, animation frames, and skill effect assets.
